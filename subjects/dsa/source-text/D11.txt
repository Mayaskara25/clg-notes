           Threaded Binary Trees
                (Continued)
Threaded Binary Tree is a binary tree in which all left
child pointers that are NULL (Left in-threaded binary
tree) points to its in-order predecessor,

 and all right child pointers that are NULL (Right
in-threaded binary tree) points to its in-order successor.
Such pointers are called threads.

Advantages:
∙ To make inorder traversal faster and
  do it without stack and without
  recursion.

∙ The threads are also useful for fast
  accessing ancestors of a node.

 Types of Threaded Binary Trees.
Single Threaded: Where a NULL right pointers of a
node is made to point to the inorder successor
Similarly, Where a NULL left pointers of a node can
be used to point to its inorder predecessor.
Double Threaded: Where both left and right NULL
pointers are made to point to inorder predecessor and
inorder successor respectively.
