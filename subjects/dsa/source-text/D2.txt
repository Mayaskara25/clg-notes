Linear Queue: Space Utilization Problem

  After deletions, free cells may remain at the beginning while rear reaches the end.

             0             1          2           3           4            5

                                      C          D            E            F

        unused positions                                          rear at end

  The simple array representation can require movement of elements to reclaim space.
  The worst-case cost of shifting can be O(n).
  The circular queue eliminates repeated shifting by wrapping around.
